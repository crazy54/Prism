Exit-Code Prompt  ops-prompt-exitcode
A prompt that shows the last exit status: a typo gives x 127, Ctrl-C gives x
130, curl -f gives x 22, and a clean run gives a green v.
tui - 80x14 (min 60x5) - 24 s loop - ascii - 16 colors
tags: prompt, exit-code, ps1, bash, troubleshooting, curl
