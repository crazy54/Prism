Concentric Rings  progress-concentric-rings
Three nested rings for the three phases of a rolling deploy, each with a legend
row giving its count, so color is never the only cue.
progress - 52x11 (min 24x3) - 11 s loop - braille - 256 colors
tags: rings, activity, nested, rollout, braille
