Text on Fire  text-fire
The letters are the fuel: every ink pixel feeds a rising flame field, so fire
licks off the tops and edges of the word.
text - 72x20 (min 36x10) - 6 s loop - unicode - truecolor
tags: fire, flames, burning, heat, doom fire
