Menu Bar  input-menu-bar
A Turbo Vision-style menu bar with hotkey letters: F10 drops a shadowed menu,
the highlight walks items past a disabled one, a submenu flies out, Esc closes.
tui - 64x14 (min 40x11) - 6 s loop - unicode - 16 colors
tags: menu bar, dropdown, turbo vision, submenu, hotkeys
