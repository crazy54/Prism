      01 / How it works·Three things to know
      The game tells your deck.
      No screen reading, no guesswork: Rocket League says what is happening, and Prism Deck lights it.
      
      
        
        
      1. 
          
        01 / Official
        
          
        Psyonix’s own Stats API.
        
          
        Rocket League reports your match on your own computer through its official Stats API (127.0.0.1). You edit nothing by hand: Connect Rocket League in Prism Deck sets the Stats API’s ini for you, then you restart the game once. No mods, no overlays, nothing touches the game’s memory.
        
        
        2. 
        
      2. 
          
        02 / Fair
        
          
        Only your team’s boost.
        
          
        The Stats API withholds the other team’s boost by design, and so does Prism: only ever your team’s. Goals, saves, demolitions and the clock are what every player sees. Display only: the deck never drives the car, and player names are dropped the moment a message arrives.
        
        
        3. 
        
      3. 
          
        03 / Every deck
        
          
        Every Stream Deck, Blue or Orange.
        
          
        The Galleon 100 SD puts the score, the clock and your team’s boost on its screen, the Stream Deck + on its touch strip, and every key plays the goals and demos. The keys wear a Rocket League skin by default (arena neon on a dark pitch); Prism’s own look is one setting away. Effects are Soft by default, with Full, Calm and Still.
        
        
        4. 
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      
        
      
          
      02 / Status·Honestly
      
          
      Coming in Prism Deck 0.8.
      
          
      Rocket League support is built: the Stats API client, the normalizer you just watched and the Rocket League keys, strips and screens, covered by automated tests. What’s left is tuning it against a live match on real hardware.
      
          
      There’s no download yet: today’s plugin (0.7) doesn’t include Game Link. Everything above runs on a fictional match with the Stats API’s documented shapes.
