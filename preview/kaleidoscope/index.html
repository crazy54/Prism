01 / HOW IT WORKS·SET UP ONCE

One layout. Every app.

You don’t build pages or profiles. You pin what you always want, and Prism draws the rest from what’s on your screen.

  1. 01 / PIN ONCE

    Pin what never changes.

    Headphone volume on a dial, mic mute on a key. They stay exactly where you put them, in every app.

  2. 02 / FOLLOWS YOUR SCREEN

    It follows what’s in front.

    The app, the mode inside it (the track builder, the site, a session waiting on you) and its live state. Every other key, dial and screen redraws to match.

  3. 03 / YOUR SHORTCUTS

    It uses your shortcuts.

    Researched default layouts (15 context packs, 736 controls, 191 cited sources), adapted to your own rebinds in VS Code, Windows Terminal and Planet Coaster 2.

  4. 04 / STAYS PUT

    Muscle memory, kept.

    The same app gives the same keys in the same places, every time. It never swaps a key under your finger.

03 / ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT·HELP WANTED

Help build it.

Kaleidoscope is only as good as its database of apps. Every app needs a pack: its controls and default shortcuts, ready for the deck. The more apps people log, the more apps work the moment they’re in front.

We’re building an opt-in tool that logs app data on your PC and sends it to that database. It isn’t out yet. Sign up now to log and send app data, and we may give you nightly build access.

Sign up for nightly access

Opens a prefilled issue on GitHub (crazy54/Prism) asking for your devices, the apps you use and your OS. Nothing is created until you submit it, and issues are public, so leave out anything private.

  • Opt-in only.

    It’s off until you turn it on, one kind of data at a time.

  • You see it first.

    A local outbox holds everything for 48 hours before it sends. Review it, or delete it.

  • About apps, never about you.

    It never sends window titles, file names, URLs, what you type, screen pixels or account ids.

  • Packs for everyone.

    The packs built from it are published CC0, free for anyone to use.

05 / PRIVACY AND SAFETY·BY DESIGN

Smart, not sneaky.

A deck that redraws itself has to be trusted. These rules are built in, not settings.

  • Streamer-safe.

    Streamer-safe mode turns on by itself while OBS runs, and it’s enforced inside the browser, so hidden titles never leave it.

  • Anti-cheat-safe.

    No game feed and no screen reading for online competitive games. Screen reading is for allow-listed offline games only; CS2 lights up through Valve’s own read-only feed.

  • Only your press.

    Input goes out only in direct response to your own press or turn. It re-checks the window before every keystroke and never acts on a doubtful reading.

  • Nothing without a click.

    Nothing is installed or written to your system without a click, every write has a safe mode, and there’s no memory reading or injection into games.

07 / STATUS·HONESTLY

Coming soon. Built in the open.

Prism Kaleidoscope is in active development. The engine, 15 context packs, Prism Sight and the browser, Windows and AI modules are built and tested in software, with four independent security and correctness reviews whose findings are all fixed with regression tests. Hardware testing on the Galleon 100 SD and the Stream Deck + is under way, then the Planet Coaster 2 calibration and a live CS2 match. Until that’s done, nothing here is verified on real hardware.
