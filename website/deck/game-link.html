    01 / HOW IT WORKS·THREE THINGS TO KNOW
    The game tells your deck.
    No screen reading, no guesswork: Counter-Strike 2 says what is happening, and Prism Deck lights it.
    
      
      
        
    1. 
          
      01 / OFFICIAL
      
          
      Valve’s own Game State Integration.
      
          
      CS2 has a built-in, supported feed for exactly this. Connect CS2 in Prism Deck adds one small config file to the game’s cfg folder, and from then on the game posts its state to Prism on your own computer (127.0.0.1). It’s the same feed gaming peripherals use for their game lighting: no mods, no overlays, nothing touches the game’s memory.
      
        
      2. 
        
    2. 
          
      02 / READ-ONLY
      
          
      It only listens.
      
          
      Prism reads what your own HUD already shows you: health, armor, ammo, money, the round. It never sends anything to the game, and your Steam ID and name are dropped the moment a post arrives. Live players don’t get a bomb timer, so neither does your deck: a planted bomb pulses at a fixed beat, with no countdown, by design.
      
        
      3. 
        
    3. 
          
      03 / EVERY DECK
      
          
      Every Stream Deck, and the Galleon’s screen.
      
          
      One profile per device. The Corsair Galleon 100 SD puts your vitals on its screen, the Stream Deck + on its touch strip, and the MK.2, XL and Mini fit everything on keys. Effects are Soft by default; Full, Calm and Still are one setting away, and spectating dims the deck to your own last numbers.
      
        
      4. 
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
    
          
    02 / STATUS·HONESTLY
    
          
    Coming in Prism Deck 0.8.
    
          
    Game Link is built: the local listener, the CS2 normalizer you just watched, and twelve Game Link keys, strips and screens, covered by more than fifty automated tests. What’s left is tuning it against a live match on real hardware, starting with the Galleon 100 SD and the Stream Deck +.
    
          
    There’s no download yet: today’s plugin (0.7) doesn’t include Game Link. Everything above runs on a fictional match with recorded CS2 shapes.
