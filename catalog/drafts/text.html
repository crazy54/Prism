🧊 3D & Extrude — real perspective, rotation and depth

Prism
Spin-3D
.ntx2-spin
The whole word rotates a full turn on the Y axis in real CSS 3D (preserve-3d + rotateY), like a swinging sign. Best on short display words.

Extrude-Solid
.ntx2-extrude
A ladder of colored offset shadows builds a solid extruded body while the block tilts left-right in perspective, so you see the 3D side face. Chunky hero titles.
STREAMING
Flip-In (per letter)
.ntx2-flipin
Each letter swings down into place from a −90° rotateX on a stagger, like flaps dropping flat. Add tx-perletter data-split so the splitter wraps every glyph.
TUMBLE
Tumble-3D
.ntx2-tumble
The headline tumbles end-over-end on both X and Y axes in true 3D with a soft glow. High-energy attract-loop for a single short word.
PRISM3D·
Cylinder-Ring
.ntx2-ring
Letters sit around a rotating cylinder in real 3D (rotateY(--a) translateZ), spinning past the viewer. Give each child span a --a angle stepping around the circle.
BLOCK
Isometric-Block
.ntx2-iso
A skew plus a diagonal ladder of colored-then-dark shadows gives fat isometric block letters that gently float. Static-friendly; strong on the dark theme.
DEPTH
Depth-Parallax
.ntx2-parallax
Two dimmer clones sit behind the face at negative translateZ, so as the group tilts they parallax apart into layered depth. Requires a matching data-text.
