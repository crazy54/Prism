Drawn in light.

One hairline family for every surface of Prism. Geometric, round-capped, optically balanced on a 24px grid and drawn in currentColor. At rest they are quiet instruments; in the spectral state their glass facets fill with the gallery's hue and a white glint catches the edge.

class="f"

Facet fill — --icon-fill (none at rest), --icon-fill-opacity.

class="a"

Accent stroke / dot — --icon-accent (currentColor at rest).

class="g"

Glint — hidden at rest; --icon-glint:1 shows it in --icon-glint-color.

--icon-stroke

Stroke width (1.5). Try 1.75 at 16px for denser UIs.
