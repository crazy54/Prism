    Why "all sites": the browser lets an extension take a picture of a tab only if it has access to every site, so the browser will ask to "Read and change all your data on all websites". Prism Control uses that access only for this preview: a small picture of the active tab, at most 2 per second, only while a preview widget is on your deck, never while streamer-safe is on, and never saved. Media controls don't need it. You can remove it here at any time.
